Exports GeoPackage, GeoJSON, 3D Shapefile and 3D DXF. Copyright (C) 2026 Mohamed Hamouda. Requires NumPy and Pillow in QGIS Python; no automatic dependency installation. Supports unrotated GeoTIFF charts in EPSG:3857 or EPSG:4326 only. QGIS 3.40+ in the 3.x series; QGIS 4/Qt6 is not supported. Tested locally on Windows; other platforms remain unverified. Extracted contours and estimated depths require manual review. Working layers are temporary until exported. Licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later; provided without warranty. See LICENSE and NOTICE.
